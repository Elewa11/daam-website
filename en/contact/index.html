FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I volunteer with Da'am Foundation?
You can volunteer by filling out the join form on the "Join Us" page, or by visiting our office in Belbeis. We welcome all experiences and fields.
Do you accept in-kind donations?
Yes, we accept in-kind donations such as clothing, medical equipment, and food to distribute to eligible families after sorting and preparation.
How do you ensure aid reaches those who deserve it?
We have a specialized field research team that visits families and studies their cases carefully to ensure support reaches the most vulnerable groups under transparent criteria.
Are there certificates for volunteers?
Yes, we grant accredited experience and volunteering certificates to volunteers who actively participate in our programs and activities for a defined period.
