Our Story

The journey began in Belbeis

Da'am Foundation for Sustainable Development was born from a group of young people's belief that real development starts with empowering people in their local community. We launched from Belbeis in Sharkia governorate, officially registered No. 3924 of 2025.

We believe we are not just a development foundation, but a youth entity seeking support and empowerment to achieve fair development reaching every person. We plan to expand our impact to neighboring villages and districts and from there across the whole country.

Our Vision

To be a leading model in achieving comprehensive local sustainable development in Egypt, building an empowered community able to manage its resources and shape its future.

Our Mission

Empowering the most vulnerable groups through innovative programs in economic empowerment, education, and environmental protection, while embedding the values of volunteerism and social responsibility.

Our Values

The principles that guide our work

Transparency

We commit to the highest standards of integrity and clarity in everything we do.

Empowerment

We put people at the heart of development and give them the tools to lead change.

Sustainability

We seek lasting impact across generations, balancing economic, social, and environmental goals.

Who We Serve

Our Target Groups

Women

Vulnerable Families
