Our Programs

Three integrated pillars of sustainable development we work on to build a better future.

Home / Programs
First Program

Economic Development & Improving Livelihoods

We empower individuals economically to achieve financial independence through technical and financial support for the most vulnerable groups.

  • Entrepreneurship & small businesses: Training and mentoring to support ideas and startups.
  • Financial inclusion & job readiness: Skills to integrate into the formal economy.
  • Handicrafts & heritage support: Preserving heritage as a source of income.
Second Program

Social & Cultural Development

We seek to enhance social solidarity and cultural expression and support the groups most in need of care.

  • Education support: High-quality educational opportunities for children and youth.
  • Health & population: Initiatives to raise health awareness and improve services.
  • Protection & solidarity: Safety nets for vulnerable groups.
Third Program

Environmental Development & Climate Change

We tackle environmental challenges and promote sustainability through practical initiatives and projects.

  • Climate change mitigation: Innovative solutions to reduce carbon emissions.
  • Pollution control: Campaigns and projects to reduce pollution of all kinds.
  • Waste management & recycling: Effective systems and rational use of natural resources.
